# Statutory Authority Directory (India)

## Overview

`packages/core/domain/authorityRegistry.js` maintains a verified directory of official Indian regulatory and grievance escalation bodies. Every entry is verified against current statutory provisions and portal attachment specifications as of November 2026.

## Detailed Regulatory Profiles

### 1. UGC e-Samadhaan (`samadhaan.ugc.ac.in`)
* **Legal Mandate:** University Grants Commission (Redressal of Grievances of Students) Regulations, 2023.
* **Jurisdiction:** All Central, State, Private, and Deemed Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) across India.
* **Statutory Resolution Window:** 20 working days for student grievances.
* **Key Use Cases:** Hostel living condition hazards, rat infestations, unhygienic washrooms, administrative inertia, and ignored warden memos.
* **Required Data:** Student Name, ABC ID / Enrolment ID, Institution Name, State, Detailed Chronology, Prior Warden Memos.

### 2. FSSAI Food Safety Connect (`foscos.fssai.gov.in/consumergrievance/`)
* **Legal Mandate:** Section 59 & Section 65 of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006.
* **Jurisdiction:** Food business operators, campus mess contractors, hostel caterers, and canteens.
* **Attachment Constraint:** Strictly JPG/PNG image formats. PDF files are rejected by the portal.
* **Key Use Cases:** Contaminated mess food, insect/worm contamination, unhygienic drinking water, unlicensed contractors.

### 3. National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (`cybercrime.gov.in`)
* **Legal Mandate:** Information Technology Act, 2000 read with applicable provisions of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) 2023.
* **Jurisdiction:** Cyberstalking, electronic harassment, non-consensual image distribution, and impersonation.
* **Key Feature:** **Anonymous Reporting Option for Women**, generating an official acknowledgment tracking number.
* **Toll-Free Helpline:** 1930 (Centralized National Cyber Financial & Cybercrime Helpline).

### 4. College Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)
* **Legal Mandate:** Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 read with UGC Saksham Guidelines 2015.
* **Statutory Inquiry Period:** 90 days from complaint receipt.
* **Submission Mode:** Authorized email from student's verified academic email account to the Presiding Officer.

### 5. National Anti-Ragging Monitoring Agency (`antiragging.in`)
* **Legal Mandate:** UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions, 2009.
* **Protocol:** Zero-tolerance immediate escalation protocol. Bypasses standard internal college hierarchy.
* **24/7 Toll-Free Helpline:** 1800-180-5522. Complaints can be lodged confidentially.
